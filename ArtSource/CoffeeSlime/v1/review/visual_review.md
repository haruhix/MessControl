# Coffee slime DCC visual review

Reviewed actual PNGs: original generated concept, graybox front/left/hero, polished hero, front/back/left/right/top/gameplay view sheet, final fresh FBX/GLB appearance, and critical action poses. The original coffee silhouette, crema curl and large ivory/mauve eyes read together from the higher review camera. Closed body coverage and the face attachment remain visible in profile and back views; wet highlights distinguish dark coffee from matte foam without a transparent layer.

Hop poses show compressed anticipation, separated aerial footprint/shadow, stretched flight, broad landing compression and rebound. Hurt recoils briefly with a small lift that keeps the deformed footprint above the floor. Idle/Hop are cyclic; terminal PreBurst is a much larger three-dimensional balloon, and the Burst action rapidly collapses it into a thin footprint. Native GroundBurst/Droplets must supply the actual outgoing splash rather than this model generating new particles.

The first lateral-only PreBurst is retained only as intermediate evidence. It was corrected before final handoff. Final framing includes the swollen pose; the same fixed motion camera is used across actions.

The 6.58-second MP4 is a labelled review montage, 960×720 at 24 fps, 158 frames. It repeats Idle/Hop/Hurt before PreBurst/Burst. Native animation files retain exact 100-fps source timing; the preview does not redefine gameplay durations.

Limits: the coffee body is dark and intentionally opaque; acceptance of its contrast against actual coffee water needs the Unreal lighting/camera check. DCC renders use a neutral review stage. Native damage flash, collision, engine skin import, materials, paint and event timing require root-owned runtime checks. No claim of full engine parity, network correctness or measured GPU cost.

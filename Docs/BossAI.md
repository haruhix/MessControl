# Boss foundation

`AMCBossCharacter` is a replicated character with a server-owned health/phase/attack snapshot. `AMCBossAIController` makes decisions on a timer, acquires living player-controlled tooth characters, checks visibility, follows real navigation paths, telegraphs a locked attack direction, strikes once, and recovers. It remembers a lost player's last visible location briefly; it cannot attack through occluders or replace failed navigation with direct movement.

`/Game/Gameplay/Boss/BP_ZombieBoss` is placed dormant in `/Game/Maps/L_Mouth`; its profile is `/Game/Gameplay/Boss/DA_ZombieBoss`. The profile references the imported artist Zombie, its three used texture maps and an independent 85-bone skeleton. The artist's unused Bag material was excluded. The player skeleton has a different reference pose, so animation clips require retargeting; no finished Zombie AnimBP is claimed. Activate the placed boss from F3 or call `ActivateBoss` from an encounter trigger. Task rewards do not activate it automatically.

For another boss, create a Blueprint child of `AMCBossCharacter`, assign a `UMCBossProfile`, configure its capsule/mesh and place it on suitable navigation. Project SupportedAgents retain Default `35/144` and add Boss `60/220`, matching the Zombie capsule. The map contains saved navigation for both. `Tools/Unreal/create_roguelike_foundation.py` authors only tagged foundation actors; the editor helper builds the actual volume brush and drains completed asset loading before building navigation in a commandlet.

Each profile attack has a unique `AttackId`, phase gate, selection weight, range, vertical reach, cone, damage and timings. The default `ExecuteAttack` deals one frontal impact to eligible players through their authoritative tooth-status component. Override this BlueprintNativeEvent for a projectile, hazard or multi-step skill. The override owns its damage; keep authority checks and do not call the parent unless its frontal hit is intended too. Use `OnBossTelegraph`, `OnBossAttackImpact`, health/phase/state/death events for montage, audio and VFX only. Replicated server timestamps let presentation resume at the correct age instead of restarting on every update. Impact is a reliable cosmetic multicast; gameplay damage remains server-only.

Use `ReceiveBossDamage` or the engine `ApplyDamage` route to damage the boss. Attack/phase configuration is sanitized and copied at initialization; edits do not mutate an attack midway through its windup. A phase unlocks when health crosses its sorted threshold and never regresses. Deactivate, death, unpossess and EndPlay stop movement and clear timers. Death cancels a pending strike and disables the collision capsule. `ResetForRun` clears attack timers/cooldowns, restores health, phase and walking collision, and returns even a killed boss to Dormant without activating it. Handle the Dormant state event to reset Blueprint presentation.

This is the behavior/ability integration foundation. Final animation, attack VFX, encounter pacing, bespoke abilities and loot from boss death remain encounter work. The starter profile includes Claw and a half-health phase that increases movement speed and unlocks Slam.

The focused solo editor-runtime preview passed authoritative damage, living-player targeting, actual navigation movement, telegraph/impact state transitions and stopped behavior after death. The starter phase configuration was saved; a separate multiplayer or final animation pass was not performed.

# VFX lab

Open `/Game/Tests/L_VFXLab` and press Play. The default map is a clean, open test area with its own neutral game mode, lab lighting, real repeating tooth bots and real gameplay actors. It has no tongue mesh, roof or copied mouth environment. The 51 scenarios use separate flat support floors and local gameplay fixtures, including the individual teeth, walls and hazards needed for their real mechanics.

Stations activate when a human-controlled pawn or spectator comes within 3,000 cm of the nearest point of its platform bounds. They stay active until every human observer is more than 3,600 cm away. In multiplayer, nearby stations for all human clients form the active set; demonstration bots do not activate stations. Distant stations stop ticking and destroy their temporary gameplay actors while keeping completed counters. Fab gallery effects use each local viewer's distance independently. The current open-layout proximity observations are recorded in [ProximityValidationSummary.json](../Artifacts/VFXLab_20261010/ProximityValidationSummary.json).

Controls: **F3** opens/closes the navigation panel, with station, Gallery and pause/resume buttons; **Esc** also closes it. **PageUp / PageDown** selects a station and moves the camera; **R** resets that station; **P** pauses/resumes the lab; **WASD + mouse** flies around. Resume activates only stations near human viewers. Station actors also expose `SetRunning` and `Reset`. The HUD reports completed observed cycles and failures, retaining prior results when distant fixtures unload. A requested action is not counted as a successful observation; the 51-station catalogue does not mean all 51 demonstrations run at once.

Authoring helpers:

- `Tools/Unreal/make_vfx_lab_open.py` removes copied arena scenery and configures neutral open-lab lighting while preserving existing stations; run with the lab open and PIE stopped.
- `Tools/Unreal/author_vfx_lab_scenarios.py` is the default authoring helper. It places every current station enum case, flat supports and navigation fixtures after the native classes are loaded, without requiring or copying main-arena scenery.
- `Tools/Unreal/match_vfx_lab_arena.py` is deprecated and disabled by default. Explicit `--legacy-arena-copy` opt-in reproduces the previous arena scenery/look for historical comparison. It requires saved maps and stopped PIE, does not save the main arena, and is not part of the open-lab authoring flow.
- `Tools/Unreal/report_vfx_lab.py` reads real observed results from active PIE worlds into `Saved/Codex/vfx_lab_report_latest.json`.
- `Tools/Unreal/verify_vfx_lab_proximity.py` is a phased live-PIE probe with a CLI wrapper. `init`, `clear`/`place`, then `capture` (after at least two seconds of normal PIE ticks) inspect human platform distances, the active set, paused ticks, fixture cleanup and local Fab effects. Authority-local pawn placement supplies distance-only observations; actual walking input requires a human walking pawn, and distance placement alone does not establish locomotion or network transport. `before-input`/`after-input` observe actual remote-client Slate **W / PageDown / PageUp / R / P** supplied separately by the caller. The probe never dispatches Python RPCs. Snapshots are saved to `Saved/Codex/vfx_lab_proximity_latest.json`.

Native actor changes require the authorized editor Live Coding integration. Source/asset checks and a successful compile do not establish visual quality or network correctness; record actual PIE observations before claiming a scenario verified. This lab is an opt-in development map and does not change the main arena's game mode.

These checks were recorded on `L_Mouth` before the changes were combined on the latest `main`. The run IDs in the local validation folders use UTC.

- Double jump. An airborne second press reached 167 cm. With forward input it covered 3.68 m versus 2.44 m for a single jump at the same horizontal speed. A third press did not add lift. Evidence is in `DoubleJump` and `DoubleJumpForward`.
- Explosions. Rocket and timed grenade blasts broke food into the existing intake crumbs. The grenade retained its bounces and timer. Evidence is in `ExplosiveDamage`.
- Cataclysm jelly. A regular hit gave a 0.18 s reaction and splitting preserved the two intended size tiers. Evidence is in `JellyHitStun`.
- Kamikaze. The existing melee hitbox accepted a pickaxe hit. In separate blast cases, the target within the 230 cm radius took damage and the target outside it stayed at full health. This was verification of existing behavior. Evidence is in `KamikazeHitbox`.
- Respawn. The spirit accepted WASD before pull, approached the exposed crown, whitened with it, and returned control on release. Focused automation checked allocation of distinct intact reserves. Solo review used gameplay and side cameras. Evidence is in `DeathRespawn`, `DeathRebirth`, `DeathAbsorption` and `DeathApproach`. Composer exposes the editable effects. An isolated Composer scenario remains pending.
- Reserve HUD. A broken tooth fractured the icon and advanced the counter once, from 12 to 11. The affected cards rendered without their gray drop shadows. Evidence is in `ReserveHUDRevision`.
- Camera. The `PhysicalImpactFeedback` and `HeavyPropGroundImpact` tests passed. The camera stayed steady beside a damaged crown in solo PIE. Sustained pressure was exercised in automation. Evidence is in `Saved/Checks/RootedToothCamera`.
